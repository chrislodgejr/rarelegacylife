# Rare Legacy Life Brand Assets

Prepared transparent logo assets live in `public/brand`.

## Generated Assets

- `public/brand/logo-dark-horizontal.png`: white horizontal logo for black or rich-black backgrounds.
- `public/brand/logo-dark-stacked.png`: white stacked logo for black or rich-black backgrounds.
- `public/brand/logo-light-stacked.png`: black stacked logo for white or off-white backgrounds.
- `public/brand/icon-dark.png`: white icon mark for dark backgrounds.
- `public/brand/icon-light.png`: black icon mark for light backgrounds.
- `public/favicon.png`, `src/app/favicon.ico`, `src/app/icon.png`, `src/app/apple-icon.png`: favicon and app icon outputs.

The uploaded recruiting flyer source is preserved in `public/brand/originals` but is not used as a logo lockup.
